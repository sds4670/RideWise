# Transcribed scanned annexures

The two annexures below are scanned images inside the policy PDFs, not selectable
text. They were viewed as images and transcribed verbatim on 2026-10-10 so the
rules engine and the agent can rely on machine-readable text. The PDFs remain the
authoritative source.

---

## Annexure 1 — Approved refund schedule (Version 2)

Source: `data/03_Cancellation_and_Refund_Policy_v2.pdf`, page 2 (RW-POL-03, v2).
Heading on the scan: "Annexure 1: Refund schedule (Version 2)".
Applies to bookings made on or after 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST.

| Cancellation before scheduled pickup            | Refund of rental charge |
|-------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| 48 hours or more                                | 100%                    |
| 12 hours or more, but less than 48 hours        | 75%                     |
| 2 hours or more, but less than 12 hours         | 25%                     |
| Less than 2 hours                               | No refund (0%)          |

Notes on the scan:
- Cancelled by RideWise: 100% refund in all cases.
- No-show: no refund.
- Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 20 June 2026.
- Stamp: APPROVED.

## Annexure A — Tier benefits (late return grace period)

Source: `data/04_Late_Return_and_Damage_Penalty_Schedule.pdf`, page 3 (RW-POL-04, v2.1).
Heading on the scan: "Annexure A: Tier benefits".
Subtitle: "Late return grace period by customer tier".

| Customer tier | Grace period on late return              |
|---------------|------------------------------------------|
| STANDARD      | None                                     |
| SILVER        | None                                     |
| GOLD          | 30 minutes (up to and including 30 min)  |

Notes on the scan:
- A GOLD return up to 30 minutes late: no late fee.
- A GOLD return more than 30 minutes late: the full lateness is charged from the
  first minute, as for other tiers.
- Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 15 December 2024.
- Stamp: APPROVED.
